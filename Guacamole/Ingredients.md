Core Ingredients
Lime juice: Adds brightness and prevents the avocado from browning 
Tomatoes: Diced for freshness and texture 
Onions: Typically red or white, finely chopped for crunch and flavor 
Cilantro: Chopped fresh cilantro adds a bright, herbal note 
Jalapeños: Optional, for a mild to moderate heat 
Salt and pepper: To taste, enhancing all the flavors
