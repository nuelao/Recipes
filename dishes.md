# Top Dishes Around the World

## 1. Pizza Margherita 🇮🇹
Originating from Naples, Italy, this classic pizza combines a thin crust with tomato sauce, mozzarella, and fresh basil.

## 2. Sushi 🍣 🇯🇵
A Japanese delicacy featuring vinegared rice paired with fresh fish, seafood, or vegetables.

## 3. Butter Chicken 🇮🇳
Tender chicken cooked in a rich tomato and butter-based curry sauce, often served with naan or rice.

## 4. Ramen 🍜 🇯🇵
A comforting noodle soup featuring broth, noodles, meat, eggs, and vegetables.

## 5. Tacos 🌮 🇲🇽
Soft or crispy tortillas filled with seasoned meat, seafood, vegetables, and fresh toppings.

## 6. Steak Fajitas 🥩🌮 🇲🇽
Sizzling strips of steak served with sautéed onions and peppers, accompanied by warm tortillas.

## 7. Beef Wellington 🥩 🇬🇧
A premium dish consisting of beef fillet coated with mushroom duxelles and wrapped in puff pastry.

## 8. Paella 🥘 🇪🇸
A Spanish rice dish traditionally made with seafood, chicken, rabbit, and saffron.

## 9. Lasagna 🍝 🇮🇹
Layers of pasta, meat sauce, béchamel, and cheese baked until golden.

## 10. Peking Duck 🦆 🇨🇳
A famous Chinese dish known for its crispy skin and tender meat, served with pancakes and sauces.

## 11. Fish and Chips 🐟🍟 🇬🇧
Battered fried fish served alongside chunky chips, a staple of British cuisine.

## 12. Hamburger 🍔 🇺🇸
A grilled beef patty served in a bun with various toppings and sauces.

## 13. Biryani 🍚 🇮🇳
A fragrant rice dish cooked with spices and meat or vegetables.

## 14. Dim Sum 🥟 🇨🇳
A collection of small bite-sized dishes such as dumplings, buns, and rolls.

## 15. Coq au Vin 🍗 🇫🇷
A traditional French dish of chicken slowly braised in wine with mushrooms and herbs.

## 16. Pho 🍜 🇻🇳
A Vietnamese noodle soup featuring aromatic broth, herbs, rice noodles, and beef or chicken.

## 17. Shawarma 🌯 🇸🇾
Thinly sliced marinated meat roasted on a spit and served in wraps or flatbreads.

## 18. Picanha 🥩 🇧🇷
Brazil's most prized beef cut, grilled simply and seasoned with coarse salt.

## 19. Moussaka 🍆 🇬🇷
A layered casserole made with aubergine, minced meat, and béchamel sauce.

## 20. Chicken Tikka Masala 🍛 🇮🇳
Roasted chicken pieces served in a creamy spiced tomato sauce.
